MoP Classic Blacksmithing Guide - Bonuses, Recipes & Overview

This overview covers all the new Blacksmithing recipes and changes in MoP Classic, including epic crafted gear, vendor plans and reputation unlocks.

If you're looking to level up Blacksmithing, check out my separate leveling guide here:

MoP Classic Blacksmithing Leveling Guide

Blacksmithing Perk in MoP

Blacksmiths can add extra sockets to their own bracers and gloves using the Socket Bracer and Socket Gloves spells.

You can use any regular gem in these sockets, so it's flexible based on your class and spec. This gives you:

  • +320 Strength, Agility, or Intellect
  • +480 Stamina
  • +640 of any secondary stat (Spirit, Parry, Haste, Mastery, Crit, etc.)

Blacksmithing Trainers

You can learn Blacksmithing all the way up to 600 from any regular profession trainer in MoP Classic, including the ones in Stormwind and Orgrimmar. There's no need to travel to Pandaria unless you prefer to.

If you still want to visit a Pandaria trainer, here’s where to find them:

  • The Jade Forest: Len the Hammer
  • Shrine of Two Moons (Horde): Jorunga Stonehoof
  • Shrine of Seven Stars (Alliance): Cullen Hammerbrow

Miscellaneous Items

  • Living Steel Weapon Chain - Reduces the duration of Disarm effects by 50%
  • Ghostly Skeleton Key - Blacksmith only item to unlock locks.
  • Living Steel Belt Buckle - Used to add sockets to belts.
  • Ghost Iron Shield Spike - Used to add a spike to shields.
Plans: Living Steel Belt Buckle is sold by Ambersmith Zikk and requires Honored reputation with The Klaxxi. It costs 20x Kyparite.

The rest of them can be learned from the Blacksmithing trainer.
