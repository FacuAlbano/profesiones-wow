MoP Classic Tailoring Guide - Bonuses, Recipes & Overview

This MoP Classic Tailoring guide gives you an overview of the profession in Mists of Pandaria Classic. It covers profession perks, daily cooldowns, key recipes, reputation gear, and rare or epic items you can craft.

If you're looking to level up Tailoring, check out my separate leveling guide here:

MoP Classic Tailoring Leveling Guide

Profession Bonuses and Perks

In MoP Classic, Tailoring gives you exclusive cloak enchants that provide a high stat proc for 15 seconds. These embroidery enchants are the main profession bonuses for Tailors and offer extra Intellect, Spirit, or Attack Power depending on your spec. You can also craft cheaper leg enchants for yourself, which give the same stats as regular ones but cost fewer materials.

Cloak Embroidery Enchants

Tailors can add exclusive embroidery enchants to their cloaks for a temporary stat boost:

  • Darkglow Embroidery - 3000 Spirit for 15 sec
  • Lightweave Embroidery - 2000 Intellect for 15 sec
  • Swordguard Embroidery - 4000 Attack Power for 15 sec

Cheap Self-Only Leg Enchants

Tailors can craft cheaper versions of leg enchants for their own gear. These have the same stats as the regular ones but only cost 3x Eternium Thread:

  • Sanctified Spellthread
  • Master's Spellthread

Daily Cooldown

Imperial Silk is the main daily cooldown for Tailoring in MoP Classic. It creates Imperial Silk, which is used for high-end crafts.

You can also make it without the cooldown using Song of Harmony, but this version costs 3x Spirit of Harmony.

What is Imperial Silk used for?

Imperial Silk is Bind on Pickup, so you can't sell the cloth itself, only the items you craft with it. However, as you'll see in the recipe lists below, every Imperial Silk recipe requires either a reputation grind or buying patterns from the Auction House. If you're thinking of leveling Tailoring on a level 85 alt just for the daily cooldown, it's probably not worth it.

This table below summarizes the main uses for Imperial Silk and how many you need for each item:

Item Type Imperial Silk Needed
Greater Spellthreads (Leg Enchants) 1
Epic Gloves (ilvl 476-496) 4
Epic Chests (ilvl 476-496) 6
Royal Satchel (28-slot bag) 12

Reputation Recipes

Most key Tailoring recipes in MoP Classic are locked behind faction reputation. These include leg enchants, epic gear, and a high-capacity bag useful for all players.

Faction Recipe / Item Reputation
Golden Lotus Leg Armor Kits (Healer, Spell DPS) Honored
Golden Lotus ilvl 476 Chest and Glove recipes Honored
The August Celestials Royal Satchel (28-slot BoE bag) Exalted

Golden Lotus Quartermaster

You can buy the recipes from the Golden Lotus quartermaster Jaluu the Generous in the Vale of Eternal Blossoms.

The August Celestials Quartermaster

You can buy the recipes from the August Celestials from the quartermaster Sage Whiteheart or Sage Lotusbloom in the Vale of Eternal Blossoms.

Epic Gear (ilvl 476-496)

Tailors can craft epic chest and glove pieces. These are great starter items for fresh level 90 characters gearing up for raids.
