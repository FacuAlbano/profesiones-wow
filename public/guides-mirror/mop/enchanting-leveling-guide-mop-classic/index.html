MoP Classic Enchanting Leveling Guide 1-600

This MoP Classic Enchanting leveling guide will show you the quickest and most efficient way to level your Enchanting skill up from 1 to 600 in Mists of Pandaria Classic.

Shopping List

Below is an approximate list of materials you will need to level Enchanting from 1 to 600. These numbers are estimates and may vary slightly depending on skill-up chance and recipe availability.

Tip: When buying Essences, don't search the full name. For example, search for just "Planar Essence" instead of "Greater Planar Essence". This way you'll see both Greater and Lesser versions. Remember, 1 Greater can be turned into 3 Lesser, so sometimes it's much cheaper to buy the Greater ones.

Enchanting Macro for Faster Leveling

I highly recommend using this macro to speed up the leveling process.

This macro casts the selected enchant and automatically applies it to an Enchanting Vellum in your bags, then confirms the prompt without any extra clicks.

Macro

/run DoTradeSkill(TradeSkillFrame.selectedSkill)
/use Enchanting Vellum
/script StaticPopup1Button1:Click()

How to Use

Make sure you have Enchanting Vellum in your bags. You can buy them from Enchanting Supply vendors. Don't buy them from the Auction House!

  1. Place the macro on your action bar
  2. Open your Enchanting profession window
  3. Select the enchant you want to craft (make sure it's the right one)
  4. Press the macro repeatedly

This method lets you level much faster without having to click the enchant and vellum manually each time.

Leveling MoP Enchanting

1 - 50

Blood Elf characters have +10 Enchanting skill because of their passive Arcane Affinity. An extra 10 Enchanting skill means recipes stay orange for 10 more points, so you can save a lot of gold by doing lower level recipes for 10 more points.

  • 1-2
    1x Runed Copper Rod - 1 Copper Rod, 1 Strange Dust, 1 Lesser Magic Essence

    Copper Rod is sold by Trade Good, Trade Supply, and Enchanting Supply vendors, don't buy the rod from the Auction House. The vendors are usually near your trainer somewhere.

  • 2-50
    Disenchant green quality items or make the following:

    48x Enchant Bracer: Minor Health - 48 Strange Dust

Journeyman Enchanting

Visit a trainer to learn Journeyman Enchanting. (Requires level 10)

50 - 135

  • 50-90
    40x Enchant Bracer: Minor Health - 40 Strange Dust

    If Strange Dust is cheap and Greater Magic Essence is expensive on your realm, you can make this Enchant up to 120.

  • 90-100
    10x Enchant Bracer: Minor Stamina - 30 Strange Dust
  • 100-110
    10x Greater Magic Wand - 10 Simple Wood, 10 Greater Magic Essence

    Simple Wood is sold by Trade Good, Trade Supply, and Enchanting Supply vendors, don't buy it from the Auction House. The vendors are usually near your trainer somewhere.

  • 110-135

    Buy these two recipes: Formula: Enchant Bracer - Lesser Strength and Formula: Enchant Cloak - Minor Agility from Kulwia (Horde) in Stonetalon Mountains, or from Dalria (Alliance) in Ashenvale.

    These are limited supply recipes. If someone bought them before you, you'll have to wait for them to respawn. I don't know the exact timer, but it's around 30-50 minutes. (or just grab them from the Auction House if not too Expensive)

    You'll need one of them for this step:

    25x Enchant Cloak - Minor Agility - 25x Lesser Astral Essence

    Alternatives:

    Craft one of these instead if you can’t get the recipe or don’t want to bother visiting the vendor:

    28x Enchant 2H Weapon - Minor Impact - 112x Strange Dust, 28x Small Glimmering Shard
    30x Enchant Shield - Minor Stamina - 30x Lesser Astral Essence, 60x Strange Dust

Expert Enchanting

Visit a trainer to learn Expert Enchanting. (Requires level 20)

135 - 225

    20x Enchant Bracer - Lesser Stamina - 40 Soul Dust
  • 155-185

    40x Enchant Bracer - Lesser Strength - 80 Soul Dust

    Make these instead if you couldn't get the recipe:

    2. Craft 21x Enchant Chest - Greater Health - 63 Soul Dust
    3. Craft 4x Enchant Boots - Lesser Stamina - 16 Soul Dust
  • 185-220
    45x Enchant Bracer: Strength - 45 Vision Dust

    • The enchant will be yellow, so you might need to make more.

  • 220-225
    5x Enchant Cloak: Greater Defense - 15 Vision Dust

Burning Crusade Classic (300-350)

Visit a trainer to learn Master Enchanting. (Requires level 50)

300 - 350

    10x Enchant Chest - Restore Mana Prime - 20x Lesser Planar Essence, 20x Arcane Dust
  • 310-316
    6x Enchant Bracer - Major Intellect - 18x Lesser Planar Essence
  • 316-320
    4x Gloves - Assault - 24 Arcane Dust
  • 320-330

    Make one of these, depending on whether Arcane Dust or Greater Planar Essence is cheaper on the Auction House:

    13x Enchant Gloves - Assault - 104 Arcane Dust
    10x Enchant Chest - Major Spirit - 20 Greater Planar Essence

  • 330-340

    10x Enchant Shield - Resilience - 10x Large Prismatic Shard, 40x Lesser Planar Essence

    Free 335-345 for Blood Elves

    There is an alternative cheap method for the 335-345 part, but only Blood Elves can benefit from it.

    Click Here if you have a Blood Elf

    Your racial passive skill Arcane Affinity allows you to gain ten free skill ups by converting a Large Prismatic Shard into three Small Prismatic Shard and back again.

    Only the conversion from one Large Prismatic Shard to three Small Prismatic Shards is affected by this. Therefore, you will only have the chance to gain skill-ups half of the time (since the other half of the time, you'll be converting back to three Small Prismatic Shards). The recipe will be grey, and you won't gain skill points for every craft! The chance of gaining skill points is very low. It will probably take 10-15 minutes to gain the 10 free skill points.

    The Formula: Large Prismatic Shard is sold by these NPCs, and the Small Prismatic Shard recipe is taught by your trainer.

  • 340-350
    13x Superior Wizard Oil - 39 Arcane Dust, 13 Nightmare Vine, 13 Crystal Vial

    The recipe is sold by Madame Ruby at Shattrath City. It's yellow already when you learn it, so you might need to make a few more.

    You can buy Crystal Vial from trade supply or alchemy supply vendors.

    Make more Shield - Resilience if you don't have Nightmare Vine.

MoP Classic Enchanting (500 - 600)

Visit your trainer to learn the new MoP Classic Enchanting skill. You don't have to go to Pandaria to learn the MoP Enchanting skill or recipes, any Enchanting trainer in the old capital cities can teach it.

If you still want to find the MoP Enchanting trainer in Pandaria, talk to Lai the Spellpaw in The Jade Forest.

500 - 600

  • 552-556
    Make 2 Ring Enchants - 4 Spirit Dust
  • 556-577
    7x Enchant Chest - Superior Stamina - 28 Spirit Dust, 7 Mysterious Essence

Darkmoon Faire Free +5 Profession Skill

Check your Calendar in-game to see if the Darkmoon Faire is open. The event is available every two weeks. It starts on Sunday and lasts for a week.

During the event, you can complete quests for each profession and gain +5 skill points for each. Click here to read more about the quests.

  • 577-600
    5x Enchant Chest - Glorious Stats - 10 Spirit Dust, 15 Mysterious Essence
