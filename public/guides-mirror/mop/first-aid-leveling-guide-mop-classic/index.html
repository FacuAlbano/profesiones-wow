MoP Classic First Aid Leveling Guide 1-600

This MoP Classic First Aid leveling guide will show you the fastest way how to get your First Aid skill up from 1 to 600 in Mists of Pandaria Classic.

Shopping List

  • 119x Linen Cloth
  • 71x Wool Cloth
  • 89x Silk Cloth
  • 50x Mageweave Cloth
  • 50x Runecloth
  • 50x Netherweave Cloth
  • 76x Frostweave Cloth
  • 75x Embersilk Cloth
  • 124x Windwool Cloth

First Aid Trainers

Alliance trainers:

  • Angela Leifeld in Stormwind City
  • Dannelor in Darnassus.
  • Nissa Firestone in Ironforge.
  • Nus in The Exodar.
  • Michelle Belle in Elwynn Forest.
  • Byancie in Teldrassil.
  • Thamner Pol in Dun Morogh.

Horde trainers:

  • Krenk Choplimb in Orgrimmar.
  • Pand Stonebinder in Thunder Bluff.
  • Mary Edras in Undercity.
  • Alestus in Silvermoon City.
  • Nurse Neela in Tirisfal Glades.
  • Rawrk in Durotar.
  • Vira Younghoof in Mulgore.

Leveling First Aid

1 - 75

  • 1 - 40
    50x Linen Bandage - 50 Linen cloth
  • 40 - 75
    35x Heavy Linen Bandage - 70 Linen Cloth

Journeyman First Aid

Visit your trainer and learn Journeyman First Aid.

75 - 145

  • 75 - 80
    5x Heavy Linen Bandage - 10 Linen Cloth
  • 80 - 115
    35x Wool Bandage - 35 Wool Cloth
  • 115 - 145
    15x Heavy Wool Bandage - 30 Wool Cloth

Expert First Aid

Learn Expert First Aid from your trainer.

145 - 210

  • 145 - 151
    3x Heavy Wool Bandage - 6 Wool Cloth
  • 151 - 180
    29x Silk Bandage - 29 Silk Cloth
  • 180 - 210
    30x Heavy Silk Bandage - 60 Silk Cloth

Artisan First Aid

Learn Artisan First Aid from your trainer. (Requires level 35)

210 - 300

  • 210 - 240
    30x Mageweave Bandage - 30 Mageweave Cloth
  • 240 - 260
    10x Heavy Mageweave Bandage - 20 Mageweave Cloth
  • 260 - 290
    30x Runecloth Bandage - 30 Runecloth
  • 290 - 300
    10x Heavy Runecloth Bandage - 20 Runecloth

TBC

Learn Master First Aid from your trainer. (Requires level 50)

300 - 350

  • 300 - 330
    30x Netherweave Bandage - 30 Netherweave Cloth
  • 330 - 350
    10x Heavy Netherweave Bandage - 20 Netherweave Cloth

WotLK

Learn Grand Master First Aid from your trainer. (Requires level 65)

350 - 426

  • 350 - 390
    40x Frostweave Bandage - 40 Frostweave Cloth
  • 390 - 426
    18x Heavy Frostweave Bandage - 36 Frostweave Cloth

Cataclysm

Learn Illustrious Grand Master First Aid from your trainer. (Requires level 75)

426 - 501

  • 426 - 475
    49x Embersilk Bandage - 49 Embersilk Cloth
  • 475 - 501
    13x Heavy Embersilk Bandage - 26 Embersilk Cloth

MoP

Visit any First Aid trainer in the old capital cities to learn the MoP Classic First Aid skill. You don't need to go to Pandaria to learn the skill or recipes.

If you still want the Pandaria trainers, talk to Soraka or Elder Muur in The Jade Forest.

501 - 600

  • 501 - 550
    49x Windwool Bandage - 49 Windwool Cloth
  • 550 - 600
    25x Heavy Windwool Bandage - 75 Windwool Cloth
