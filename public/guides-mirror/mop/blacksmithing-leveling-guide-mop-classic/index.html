MoP Classic Blacksmithing Leveling Guide 1-600

This MoP Classic Blacksmithing leveling guide will show you the quickest and most efficient way to level your Blacksmithing skill up from 1 to 600 in Mists of Pandaria Classic.

Shopping List

Below is an approximate list of materials you will need to level Blacksmithing from 1 to 600. These numbers are estimates and may vary slightly depending on skill-up chance and recipe availability.

Classic (1-300)

  • 150x Rough Stone
  • 100x Copper Bar
  • 130x Coarse Stone
  • 7x Silver Bar
  • 135x Bronze Bar
  • 129x Heavy Stone
  • 13x Gold Bar
  • 190x Iron Bar
  • 190x Steel Bar - (Buy 190 Iron Bar instead if you have Mining. You can make Steel Bars with Coal (Vendor).)
  • 10x Citrine
  • 50x Solid Stone
  • 150x Mageweave Cloth
  • 240x Mithril Bar
  • 20x Dense Stone
  • 420x Thorium Bar
  • 80x Rugged Leather or 10x Star Ruby

TBC (300-350)

  • 170x Fel Iron Bar
  • 10x Netherweave Cloth
  • 90x Adamantite Bar

WotLK (350-425)

  • 298x Cobalt Bar
  • 183x Saronite Bar
  • 22x Crystallized Air
  • 5x Titanium Bar

Cataclysm (425-500)

  • 166x Obsidium Bar
  • 84x Elementium Bar
  • 54x Volatile Earth
  • 8x Volatile Fire

MoP (500-600)

  • 238x Ghost Iron Bar
  • 1x Spirit of Harmony

Blacksmithing Trainers

Horde trainers:

  • Opuno Ironhorn in Orgrimmar.
  • James Van Brunt in Undercity.
  • Karn Stonehoof in Thunder Bluff
  • Bemarrin in Silvermoon City

Alliance trainers:

  • Bengus Deepforge in Ironforge.
  • Therum Deepforge in Stormwind City.
  • Miall in The Exodar.

Leveling MoP Classic Blacksmithing

Buy a Blacksmith Hammer from a Blacksmithing Supply vendor or Trade Supply vendor. They are usually near your trainer somewhere.

1 - 75

  • 1 - 30
    40x Rough Sharpening Stone - 40 Rough Stone
  • 30 - 70
    55x Rough Grinding Stone - 110 Rough Stone

    Save 10 Rough Grinding Stones.

  • 70 - 75
    10x Coarse Sharpening Stone - 10 Coarse Stone

Journeyman Blacksmithing

Visit a trainer to learn Journeyman Blacksmithing. (Requires level 10)

75 - 125

  • 75 - 95
    60x Coarse Grinding Stone - 120 Coarse Stone

    Save the Coarse Grinding Stones.

  • 95 - 100
    5x Runed Copper Belt - 50 Copper Bar
  • 100 - 105
    7x Silver Skeleton Key - 7x Silver Bar, 7x Rough Grinding Stone

    You can skip this part and make Runed Copper Belt if you don't have any Silver Bar.

  • 105 - 110
    5x Runed Copper Belt - 50 Copper Bar
  • 110 - 125
    15x Rough Bronze Shoulders - 75x Bronze Bar, 15x Coarse Grinding Stone

Expert Blacksmithing

Visit a trainer to learn Expert Blacksmithing. (Requires level 20)

125 - 225

  • 125 - 135
    15x Heavy Sharpening Stone - 15x Heavy Stone
  • 135 - 145
    38x Heavy Grinding Stone - 114x Heavy Stone

    Keep the Heavy Grinding Stones.

  • 145 - 150
    5x Patterned Bronze Bracers - 30x Bronze Bar, 12x Coarse Grinding Stone
  • 150-160
    13x Golden Skeleton Key - 13x Gold Bar, 13x Heavy Grinding Stone
  • 160 - 165
    5x Green Iron Leggings - 40 Iron Bar, 5 Heavy Grinding Stone, 5 Green Dye

    Green Dye is sold by Tailoring and Leatherworking supply vendors.

  • 165 - 190
    25x Green Iron Bracers - 150 Iron Bar, 25 Green Dye
  • 190 - 200
    10x Golden Scale Bracers - 50 Steel Bar, 20 Heavy Grinding Stone

Buy Plans: Mithril Spurs from the Auction House if you can find it cheap. Unfortunately, it's often snatched up and reposted for ridiculous prices.

This recipe saves you 90 Mithril Bars, so if the price is lower than that, it's worth buying.

  • 200 - 207

    10x Solid Grinding Stone - 40 Solid Stone

    Keep all of these.

  • 207-210

    10x Solid Sharpening Stone - 10 Solid Stone

    Make this until it turns grey.

    If you were able to get the Plans: Mithril Spurs, buy another 135 Solid Stone and make 45 Solid Grinding Stones instead of the Sharpening Stone.

  • 210 - 225
    15x Heavy Mithril Gauntlet - 90 Mithril Bar, 60 Mageweave Cloth

Artisan Blacksmithing

Visit a trainer to learn Artisan Blacksmithing. (Requires level 35)

  • 225 - 235
    10x Steel Plate Helm - 140 Steel Bar, 10 Citrine, 10 Solid Grinding Stone
  • 235 - 250

    If you have the Plans: Mithril Spurs don't accidentally start making Mithril Coif.

    15x Mithril Coif - 150 Mithril Bar, 90 Mageweave Cloth

    Alternative recipe:

    15x Mithril Spurs - 60 Mithril Bar, 45 Solid Grinding Stone

  • 250 - 260
    20x Dense Sharpening Stone - 20 Dense Stone
  • 260 - 275
    15x Thorium Bracers - 120 Thorium Bar
  • 275 - 290
    15x Imperial Plate Bracers - 180 Thorium Bar
  • 290 - 300

    Craft 10 from one of these two:

    10x Thorium Boots - 120 Thorium Bar, 80 Rugged Leather
    10x Thorium Helm - 120 Thorium Bar, 10 Star Ruby

Master Blacksmithing

Visit a trainer to learn Master Blacksmithing. (Requires level 50)

300 - 350

  • 300 - 305
    7x Fel Weightstone - 7 Fel Iron Bar, 7 Netherweave Cloth

    You might need to make more of these because you won't gain a skill point for every craft.

  • 305 - 316
    11x Fel Iron Plate Belt - 44 Fel Iron Bar

    Make 16x Thorium Leggings up to 321 if 190x Thorium Bar is cheaper than 69x Fel Iron Bar.

  • 316 - 321
    5x Fel Iron Chain Gloves - 25 Fel Iron Bar
  • 321 - 325
    4x Fel Iron Plate Boots - 24 Fel Iron Bar
  • 325 - 335
    45x Lesser Rune of Warding - 45 Adamantite Bar

    It's green for the last 5 points, so 45 is an approximate number. You might need to make more.

  • 335 - 340
    7x Fel Iron Chain Tunic - 63 Fel Iron Bar

    You might need to make more because the recipe is yellow.

  • 340 - 350
    45x Lesser Ward of Shielding - 45 Adamantite Bar

    The recipe is sold by Rohok in Hellfire Peninsula and by Mari Stonehand in Shadowmoon Valley. It's a limited supply item, so if someone bought it before you, you'll have to wait 15-60 minutes for it to respawn.

    The recipe stays green for the last 5 skill points, so the suggested amount (45) is approximate. You may need to craft more or fewer.

    If you can't get the recipe, you can instead buy Plans: Adamantite Dagger or Plans: Adamantite Cleaver from Aaron Hollman in Shattrath City. They are also sold by these NPCs: Arras, Eriden.

    12x Adamantite Dagger - 84x Adamantite Bar, 24x Knothide Leather
    12x Adamantite Cleaver - 96x Adamantite Bar

MoP Classic Blacksmithing (500-600)

  • 552 - 557
    1x Spiritguard Shield - 9 Ghost Iron Bar
  • 557 - 567

    You might need to make more of these if you're unlucky with the skill gains.

Darkmoon Faire Free +5 Profession Skill

Check your Calendar in-game to see if the Darkmoon Faire is open. The event is available every two weeks. It starts on Sunday and lasts for a week.

During the event, you can complete quests for each profession and gain +5 skill points for each. Click here to read more about the quests.

575 - 600

At 575, you will need to buy a vendor recipe to reach 600. These recipes are sold by vendors in the Vale of Eternal Blossoms, and each one costs 1x Spirit of Harmony. You can get Spirit of Harmony by combining 10x Mote of Harmony, which drop from all mobs in Pandaria, including dungeon enemies.

Unlocking Vale of Eternal Blossom

You can enter the Vale of Eternal Blossoms after completing the quest A Celestial Experience (requires level 87).

Once you finish the quest, you can find the Blacksmithing recipe vendors here:

  • Horde: Jorunga Stonehoof in Shrine of Two Moons
  • Alliance: Cullen Hammerbrow in Shrine of Seven Stars

Recipe and Materials

You only need one recipe to finish leveling.

Pick any Contender's Armor recipe, as they all give the same number of skill points. Some require more Ghost Iron Bars, but they also give more skill-ups per craft.

You will need around 58-64x Ghost Iron Bars to reach 600.
