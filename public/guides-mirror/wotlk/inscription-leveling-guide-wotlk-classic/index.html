WotLK Classic Inscription Leveling Guide 1-450

This WotLK Classic Inscription leveling guide will show you the fastest way how to level your Inscription skill up from 1 to 450 as inexpensively as possible in Wrath of the Lich King Classic.

I recommend trying Zygor's 1-80 Leveling Guide if you are still leveling your character or you just started a new alt. The guide will help you to reach level 80 a lot faster.

Herbs Required for 1-450

Milling is an ability you get when you learn Inscription. Milling can be used to mill 5 herbs for 2 - 4 pigments, then you use the pigments to turn into inks.

The first row in the table below is the total amount of herb needed, it doesn't have to be all from the same herb.

Leveling WotLK Classic Inscription

1 - 75

  • 1 - 18
    17x Ivory Ink - 17 Alabaster Pigment
  • 18 - 35
    17x Scroll of Stamina - 17 Ivory Ink, 17 Light Parchment
  • 35 - 53
    22x Moonglow Ink - 44 Alabaster Pigment
  • 53 - 75
    22x Armor Vellum - 22 Moonglow Ink, 44 Light Parchment

75 - 125

Learn Inscription Journeyman. (Requires level 10)

Multiple Glyphs and Scrolls at the same skill ranges

There are many Glyphs and Scrolls at different skill ranges with the same amount of materials needed, so when you see [Glyph of ...], you can choose between more recipes that are orange to you and requires the same amount of materials.

  • 75 - 80
    21x Midnight Ink - 42 Dusky Pigment
  • 80 - 100
    20x [Glyph of....] - 20 Midnight Ink, 20 Light Parchment

    Make any Glyph that is orange to you. Glyphs turn yellow after 10 points here, then learn new recipes and choose another Glyph.

  • 100 - 105
    40x Lion's Ink - 80 Golden Pigment

    Make any orange Glyph if you did not reach 105 by making these.

  • 105 - 125
    20x [Glyph of ...] - 20 Lion's Ink, 20 Common Parchment

    Make any Glyph that is orange to you. Your aim should always be to make glyphs while they are orange. Glyphs turn yellow after 5 points, then learn new recipes and choose another Glyph.

125 - 200

Learn Inscription Expert. (Requires level 20)

  • 125 - 130

    Turn your Burnt Pigment into Dawnstar Ink. If you didn't mill your own herbs and you don't have Burnt Pigment, you can just make any Glyph that is orange to you.

  • 130 - 147
    17x [Glyph of ...] - 17 Lion's Ink, 17 Common Parchment

    Make any Glyph that is orange to you. Glyphs turn yellow after 5 and 10 points here, so you have to keep learnning new ones.

  • 147 - 150
    Make Strange Tarot until you reach 150. It depends on how many Dawnstar Ink you have.
  • 150 - 155
    47x Jadefire Ink - 94 Emerald Pigment
  • 155 - 195
    40x [Glyph of ...] - 40 Jadefire Ink, 40 Common Parchment

    Make orange Glyphs. You have to keep learning new ones every 5 points.

    Turn all your Indigo Pigment into Royal Ink at 175 if you have any.

  • 195 - 200
    7x [Glyph of ...] - 7 Jadefire Ink, 7 Common Parchment

    Make any Glyph that is YELLOW to you. There won't be any orange Glyps for this part.

    Make as many Arcane Tarot as you can if you made Royal Ink in the previous step.

200 - 290

Learn Inscription Artisan. (Requires level 35)

  • 200 - 205
    55x Celestial Ink - 110 Violet Pigment
  • 205 - 245
    40x [Glyph of ...] - 40 Celestial Ink, 40 Heavy Parchment

    Make Glyphs until they turn yellow, then learn new ones.

    Turn all your Ruby Pigment into Fiery Ink at 225.

  • 245- 250
    5x Weapon Vellum II - 5, Fiery Ink, 5 Celestial Ink, 10 Heavy Parchment

    Make any Glyph that is yellow to you if you didn't make Fiery Ink in the previous step or if you don't have 5 of them.

  • 250-255
    20-35x Shimmering Ink - 40-70 Silvery Pigment

    If you mill your herbs, you will probably need fewer Inks because you will make a few Ink of the Sky from your rare pigments between 275 - 295.

  • 255 - 260
    5x Scroll of Spirit V - 5 Shimmering Ink, 10 Heavy Parchment
  • 260 - 275
    15x [Glyph of ...] - 30 Shimmering Ink, 30 Heavy Parchment

    Make Glyphs until they turn yellow, then learn new ones.

  • 275 - 290
    15x Ink of the Sky - 15 Sapphire Pigment

    You have to make more orange Glyphs if you didn't mill your herbs or if you don't have 15 Sapphire Pigments.

Master Inscription

Visit a trainer at Outland and learn Master Inscription.  (Requires level 50)

You can also visit the Northrend trainers if your level is high enough.

Neferatti or Michael Schwan in Hellfire Penninsula.

Scribe Lanloer (The Scryers) and Recorder Lidio (The Aldor) in Shattrath City. It's possible you can only talk to one of the two. (depends on your reputation)

290 - 350

  • 290 - 305
    45x Ethereal Ink - 90 Nether Pigment
  • 305 - 350
    45x [Glyph of ...] - 45 Ethereal Ink, 45 Resilient Parchment

    Make Glyphs until they turn yellow, then learn new ones.

WotLK Classic Inscription

You can learn the new WotLK Classic Inscription skill from any NPCs listed below.  (Requires level 65)

Both factions can learn it from Professor Pallin at Dalaran.

Horde:

  • Booker Kells in Howling Fjord.
  • Adelene Sunlance in Borean Tundra

Alliance:

  • Mindri Dinkles in Howling Fjord.
  • Tink Brightbolt in Borean Tundra

350 - 450

  • 350 - 355
    90x Ink of the Sea - 180 Azure Pigment
  • 355 - 360
    5x Scroll of Spirit VII - 5 Ink of the Sea, 10 Resilient Parchment
  • 360 - 365
    5x Scroll of Intellect VII - 5 Ink of the Sea, 10 Resilient Parchment
  • 365 - 370
    5x Scroll of Strength VII - 5 Ink of the Sea, 10 Resilient Parchment
  • 370 - 375
    5x Scroll of Agility VII - 5 Ink of the Sea, 10 Resilient Parchment
  • 375 - 380
    Snowfall Ink - Icy Pigment

    Use all the rare pigments available. Keep the extra Snowfall Ink for Northrend Inscription Research. You will need to research to learn most "Northrend" glyphs.

  • 380 - 385
    7x Glyph of Focus - 7 Ink of the Sea, 7 Resilient Parchment
  • 385 - 386
    1x Northrend Inscription Research - 3 Ink of the Sea, 5 Resilient Parchment, Snowfall Ink
  • 386 - 400
    25x [Any Discovered Major Glyph] - 25 Ink of the Sea, 25 Resilient Parchment
  • 400 - 405
    5x Scroll of Stamina VIII - 5 Ink of the Sea, 10 Resilient Parchment
  • 405 - 410
    5x Scroll of Spirit VIII - 5 Ink of the Sea, 10 Resilient Parchment
  • 410 - 415
    5x Scroll of Intellect VIII- 5 Ink of the Sea, 10 Resilient Parchment
  • 415 - 420
    5x Scroll of Strength VIII - 5 Ink of the Sea, 10 Resilient Parchment
  • 420 - 430
    13x Scroll of Agility VIII - 13 Ink of the Sea, 26 Resilient Parchment
  • 430 - 450
    The best strategy is to stop leveling Inscription at about skill 430 and simply conduct regular Northrend Inscription Research.
