Cataclysm Classic Cooking Leveling Guide 1-525

This Cataclysm Classic Cooking leveling guide will show you the fastest way to level your Cooking skill up from 1 to 525 in Cataclysm Classic.

I recommend trying Zygor's 1-85 Leveling Guide if you are still leveling your character or you just started a new alt. The guide will help you to reach level 85 a lot faster.

Cooking Trainers

Horde trainers

  • Zamja in Orgrimmar.
  • Eunice Burch in Undercity.
  • Aska Mistrunner in Thunder Bluff.
  • Duhng in The Barrens
  • Mudduk in Stranglethorn Vale
  • Pyall Silentstride in Mulgore.
  • Sylann in Silvermoon City.

Alliance trainers

  • Stephen Ryback in Stormwind City.
  • Daryl Riknussun in Ironforge.
  • Alegorn in Darnassus.
  • Gremlock Pilsnor in Dun Morogh.
  • Tomas in Elwyn Forest.
  • Zarrin in Teldrassil
  • Mumman in The Exodar.

Cooking Leveling

If you also want to level Fishing but haven't leveled it yet, you should use my Fishing and Cooking leveling guide instead:

Fishing & Cooking Leveling Guide 1-525

1 - 40

60x Spice Bread - 60x Simple Flour, 60x Mild Spices

Cooking Supply vendors near your trainer sell these:

  • Mild Spices
  • Simple Flour
  • Refreshing Spring Water

Don't buy them from the Auction House!

The recipe will be green for the last few points, so you might have to make more.

40 - 65

Make around 25-35 from any recipes in the table below. You don't have to make the same recipe. You can make a few from each.

Food Ingredients Recipe Source
35x Roasted Boar Meat 35x Chunk of Boar Meat Trainer
25x Smoked Bear Meat 25x Bear Meat

Sold by Andrew Hilbert / Drac Roughcut

30x Spiced Wolf Meat 30x Stringy Wolf Meat Trainer
35x Brilliant Smallfish 35x Raw Brilliant Smallfish Vendors
35x Slitherskin Mackerel 35x Raw Slitherskin Mackerel Vendors

Journeyman Cooking

Learn Journeyman Cooking from your trainer. (Requires level 10 and Cooking 50)

65 - 110

You can keep making Smoked Bear Meat, but only up to around 90, so you will need around 35 Bear Meat.

Make around 65 from any of the recipes in the table below.

Food Ingredients Recipe Source
65x Boiled Clams 65x Clam Meat
65x Refreshing Spring Water (Vendor) 		Trainer
65x Coyote Steak 65x Coyote Meat Trainer
65x Longjaw Mud Snapper 65x Raw Longjaw Mud Snapper Vendors
65x Bat Bites 65x Bat Flesh Master Chef Mouldier (Horde only)
65x Strider Stew 65x Strider Meat Tari'qa (Horde only)

110 - 130

Make around 20-30 from any recipes in the table below.

Food Ingredients Recipe Source
30x Crab Cake 30x Crawler Meat Trainer
25x Dry Pork Ribs 25x Boar Ribs Trainer
20x Bristle Whisker Catfish 20x Raw Bristle Whisker Catfish Vendors
23x Cooked Crab Claw 23x Crawler Claw Kendor Kabonka (Alliance only)

Expert Cooking

Learn Expert Cooking from your trainer.

130 - 175

50x Curiously Tasty Omelet - 50 Raptor Egg

Alliance: Sold by Kendor Kabonka in Stormwind City
Horde: Sold by Nerrist in Northern Stranglethorn and by Keena in Arathi Highlands.

Alternative recipes

  • Make Bristle Whisker Catfish if you can find cheap Raw Bristle Whisker Catfish at the Auction House. The recipe is sold by these vendors.

  • Horde players can also get Recipe: Hot Lion Chops from Zargh in The Barrens at Crossroads. It's a good alternative if you can get some Lion Meat.

175 - 225

50x Roast Raptor - 50 Raptor Flesh

The Recipe: Roast Raptor is sold by Hammon Karwn and by Keena in Arathi Highlands.

Alternative recipes

  • Make Soothing Turtle Bisque if you can get some Turtle Meat.

  • Make Mithril Headed Trout if you can buy cheap Raw Mithril Head Trout. The recipe is sold by these vendors.

Artisan Cooking

Learn Artisan Cooking from your trainer.

225 - 250

Make 25 from one of the recipes below.

  • 25x Undermine Clam Chowder - 50 Zesty Clam Meat
  • 25x Spotted Yellowtail - 25 Raw Spotted Yellowtail
  • 25x Monster Omelet - 25 Giant Egg
  • 25x Tender Wolf Steak - 25 Tender Wolf Meat
  • 25x Filet of Redgill - 25 Raw Redgill

Spotted Yellowtail is the only recipe from the trainer, the rest of them are from vendors.

Recipe Source
Recipe: Undermine Clam Chowder

Sold by "Chef" Overheat in Badlands and by Mrs. Gant in Booty Bay. It's a limited supply recipe, so if someone bought it before you, you must wait for it to respawn. I'm not sure about the exact respawn timer. (or buy it from the Auction House)

Recipe: Monster Omelet

Sold by Himmik in Winterspring at Everlook. He is inside the inn
Or by Malygen (Alliance only) in Felwood.

Recipe: Tender Wolf Steak

Sold by Dirge Quikcleave in Tanaris.

Recipe: Filet of Redgill

Sold by Kelsey Yance at Booty Bay. (He is in the large building at the end of the ship dock in Booty Bay. Enter the building and make an immediate left.)

250 - 285

40x Juicy Bear Burger - 40x Bear Flank

You can also make Charred Bear Kabobs.

Alternative recipes

  • 40x Poached Sunscale Salmon - 40 Raw Sunscale Salmon
  • 40x Nightfin Soup - 40 Raw Nightfin Snapper

285 - 300

15x Smoked Desert Dumplings - 15 Sandworm Meat

You must complete two Silithus quests for this cooking recipe.

  1. Desert Recipe
  2. Sharing the Knowledge

You can farm the needed 15x Sandworm Meat from Dredge Strikers and Dredge Crushers in Silithus.

Alternative recipes

  • 15x Baked Salmon - 15x Raw Whitescale Salmon
  • 15x Lobster Stew - 15x Darkclaw Lobster

Both recipes are sold by Vivianna / Sheendra Tallgrass in Feralas.

TBC Cooking (300-350)

Learn Master Cooking from your trainer.

300 - 325

Make one of these:

30x Ravager Dog - 30x Ravager Flesh
30x Buzzard Bites - 30x Buzzard Meat

Recipe location:

Recipe: Ravager Dog is sold by Cookie One-Eye (Horde) and Sid Limbardi (Alliance) in Hellfire Peninsula.

You have to complete a quest chain for Recipe: Buzzard Bites. You can pick up the first quest from Legassi in Hellfire Penninsula. /way 49.2 74.8

  1. Ravager Egg Roundup
  2. Helboar, the Other White Meat
  3. Smooth as Butter

325 - 350

You have 3 choices now.

40x Talbuk Steak - 40x Talbuk Venison
40x Roasted Clefthoof - 40x Clefthoof Meat
40x Warp Burger - 40x Warped Flesh

Recipe location:

  • Recipe: Talbuk Steak and Recipe: Roasted Clefthoof is sold by Nula the Butcher (Horde) and by Uriku (Alliance) in Nagrand. (read the Kurenai part below if you can't talk to Uriku)
  • Recipe: Warp Burger is sold by Innkeeper Grilka (Horde) and by Supply Officer Mills in Terrokar Forest.

Kurenai Reputation

Alliance characters start as 'Unfriendly' with the Kurenai, so you can't buy recipes from Uriku if you haven't completed any Kurenai quests.

  1. Talk to Ikuti at the Orebor Harborage. Pick up Ango'rosh Encroachment and Daggerfen Deviance from him.
  2. Interact with the Wanted Poster near Ikuti and pick up the Wanted: Chieftain Mummaki quest. (if you face him, the poster is to your left)
  3. Completing these 3 quests will give you enough reputation to reach Neutral with the Kurenai faction then Uriku will sell you her cooking recipes.

There are other ways to reach neutral. This is just how I did it with my characters.

WotLK Cooking (350-450)

Learn Grand Master Cooking from your trainer.

350 - 365

Pick up the Northern Cooking quest from one of these trainers below.

  • Howling Fjord: Thomas Kolichio / Brom Brewbaster
  • Borean Tundra: Orn Tenderhoof / Rollick MacKreel

You will need 4x Chilled Meat to complete the quest and it will teach you the Northern Stew recipe. Make around 23 to reach 365

23x Northern Stew - 23 Chilled Meat

The recipe will be yellow, so you might have to make more.

365 - 400

Make around 70 from any of these recipes below.

Rhino Meat, Worm Meat, Shoveltusk Flank, or Fangtooth Herring will probably be the cheapest.

Recipe Reagents
Worm Delight Worm Meat
Shoveltusk Steak Shoveltusk Flank
Roasted Worg Worg Haunch
Rhino Dogs Rhino Meat
Mammoth Meal Chunk o' Mammoth
Smoked Salmon Glacial Salmon
Poached Nettlefish Nettlefish
Pickled Fangtooth Fangtooth Herring
Grilled Sculpin Musselback Sculpin
Baked Manta Ray Imperial Manta Ray

400 - 415

23x Black Jelly - 69x Borean Man O' War

If you can't get Borean Man O' War or it's expensive, you can make more from the previous recipe (around 70-100).

415 - 440

50x Darkbrew Lager - 100x Skin of Dwarven Stout, 50x Jug of Badlands Bourbon

You can buy the Skin of Dwarven Stout and the Jug of Badlands Bourbon from these vendors:

  • Innkeeper Firebrew in Ironforge. (Alliance)
  • Julia Gallina in Stormwind City. (Alliance)
  • Sarah Brady in Dalaran.
  • Nixxrax Fillamug in Booty bay.

440 - 450

10x Blackened Surprise - 10x Toughened Flesh

Cataclysm Classic Cooking (450-525)

Learn Illustrious Grand Master Cooking from your trainer.

Cooking Daily Quests

From this point, every recipe will be purchased with Chef's Award. This currency is rewarded for completing daily cooking quests for Marogg in Orgrimmar and for Robby Flay in Stormwind City.

Once you have enough awards, you can buy recipes from Shazdar in Orgrimmar and Bario Matalli in Stormwind City. One recipe usually costs 3x Chef's Award

450 - 475

Check the Auction House prices for the fish/meat listed below, choose the cheapest one, then buy about 20-25.

Striped Lurker - Recipe: Lightly Fried Lurker
Lavascale Catfish - Recipe: Lavascale Fillet
Blood Shrimp - Recipe: Whitecrest Gumbo
Monstrous Claw - Recipe: Seasoned Crab
Snake Eye - Recipe: Salted Eye

The easiest item to farm is Blood Shrimp if you want to farm the reagents. Blood Shrimp can be found in Abyssal Clam, which is dropped by most mobs in Vashj'ir.

475 - 500

Check the Auction House prices for the fish/meat listed below and choose the cheapest one, then buy about 30-35.

Highland Guppy - Recipe: Pickled Guppy
Blood Shrimp - Recipe: Hearty Seafood Soup
Giant Turtle Tongue - Recipe: Tender Baked Turtle

500 - 525

Same as above, check the Auction House prices for the meats and fish listed below, and choose the cheapest option. Purchase around 30-35 of that item.

Algaefin Rockfish - Recipe: Baked Rockfish
Basilisk "Liver" - Recipe: Basilisk Liverdog
Crocolisk Tail - Recipe: Beer-Basted Crocolisk
Blackbelly Mudfish - Recipe: Blackbelly Sushi
Deepsea Sagefish - Recipe: Delicious Sagefish Tail
Dragon Flank - Recipe: Grilled Dragon
Lavascale Catfish - Recipe: Lavascale Minestrone
Fathom Eel - Recipe: Skewered Eel

Alternative recipe

If you're Honored with your guild and your guild has the Set the Oven to "Cataclysmic" achievement, you can buy the Recipe: Broiled Dragon Feast from Goram or Shay Pressler for 150 gold. This saves you 3x Chef's Award token.

25x Broiled Dragon Feast - 75x Dragon Flank, 50x Delicate Wing
