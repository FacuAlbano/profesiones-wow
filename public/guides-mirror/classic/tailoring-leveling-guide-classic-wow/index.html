WoW Classic Tailoring Leveling Guide 1-300

This Classic WoW Tailoring leveling guide will show you the fastest and easiest way how to get your Tailoring skill up from 1 to 300.

Tailoring uses cloths as main materials, but you don't need any gathering profession to gather cloths, so Tailoring can be combined with any gathering profession. One other popular choice is Enchanting because you can craft a bunch of cloths, disenchant them, and use the dust and essences to level your enchanting. Check out my Classic WoW Enchanting leveling guide if you want to level Enchanting.

I recommend trying Zygor's 1-60 Leveling Guide if you are still leveling your character or just starting a new alt. It will help you to reach level 60 a lot faster.

Approximate Materials Required:

  • 204x Linen Cloth
  • 135x Wool Cloth
  • 804x Silk Cloth
  • 470x Mageweave Cloth
  • 1195x Runecloth
  • 110x Rugged Leather

Classic Tailoring Trainers

You can learn Classic Tailoring from any of these NPCs below, but you have to reach level 5 before you can learn it.

Just click on any of these links below to see the trainer's exact location. You can also walk up to a guard in any city and ask where the Tailoring trainer is, then it will be marked with a red flag on your map.

Horde trainers:

  • Snang in Orgrimmar.
  • Victor Ward in Undercity.
  • Vhan in Thunder Bluff
  • Bowen Brisboise in Tirisfal Glades.

Alliance trainers:

  • Uthrar Threx in Ironforge.
  • Lawrence Schneider in Stormwind City.
  • Trianna in Darnassus.

Journeyman Tailoring

You can only train Tailoring past 75 if you learn Journeyman Tailoring (Requires level 10). Not every tailoring trainer will teach you this skill, you will need to find an Expert Tailor.

You can learn Journeyman Tailoring from these NPCs below:

Horde trainers:

  • Magar in Orgrimmar
  • Rhiannon Davis in Undercity.
  • Tepa in Thunder Bluff.

Alliance trainers:

  • Jormund Stonebrow in Ironforge.
  • Sellandus in Stormwind City.
  • Me'lynn in Darnassus.

Expert Tailoring

You can only train Tailoring past 150 if you learn Expert Tailoring (Requires level 20). Not every tailoring trainer will teach you this skill, you will need to find an Artisan Tailor.

Horde players can learn Expert Tailoring from Josef Gregorian in Undercity and Alliance players can learn it from Georgio Bolero in Stormwind City.

Artisan Tailoring

You can only train Tailoring past 225 if you learn Artisan Tailoring (Requires level 35). Not every tailoring trainer will teach you this skill, you will need to find a Master Tailor.

Horde players can learn Artisan Tailoring from Daryl Stack in Hillsbrad Foothills at Tarren Mill. He's in the building directly to the East of the Inn.

Alliance players can learn Artisan Tailoring from Timothy Worthington in Dustwallow Marsh at Theramore Isle. He is on the top floor of a small building that is the first on the left after getting off the boat.

  • 230 - 250

260 - 280

25x Runecloth Belt - 75 Bolt of Runecloth, 25 Rune Thread

You can start making Runecloth Bag instead of Runecloth Belt if you are going to sell them at the Auction House. The bags are more expensive to make, but you can get most of your gold back. Make the belts if you don't want to bother selling that many bags.

You can find where to buy the recipe at the 280-290 part below.

280 - 290

18x Runecloth Bag - 90 Bolt of Runecloth, 36 Rugged Leather, 18 Rune Thread

Pattern: Runecloth Bag is sold by Qia in Winterspring at Everlook. She is inside the Bank building, in the left corner as soon as you walk in.

It's a limited supply recipe, so if someone bought it before you, then you have to wait for it to respawn. The restock-time of this recipe seems to be random, it can anything between 5-90 minutes. It's usually better to just buy it from the Auction House if a lot of people are camping the NPC.

Alternative

If the bag pattern is camped by players and bots, the best alternative is to make more Runecloth Belt until 285.

8x Runecloth Belt - 24x Bolt of Runecloth, 8x Rune Thread

290 or 285 - 300

18x Runecloth Gloves - 72 Bolt of Runecloth, 72 Rugged Leather, 18 Rune Thread

The recipe goes yellow at 290, get enough materials for 18 or around 23 if you start from 285.

Pattern: Runecloth Gloves is also sold by Qia in Winterspring, and it's also a limited supply recipe.

Alternative

If the pattern is camped by players or bots, here are a few alternative patterns you can buy from the Auction House.

Start by checking the price of Pattern: Runecloth Gloves. This gives you a baseline so you can compare the cost of other patterns and their materials to see if it's worth buying them instead.

Some of these patterns are yellow or will turn yellow, so you may need to craft more than 15 to reach 300.

Pattern Materials
20x Brightcloth Cloak 80x Bolt of Runecloth, 40x Gold Bar, 20x Rune Thread
15x Runecloth Pants 90x Bolt of Runecloth, 30x Ironweb Spider Silk, 15x Rune Thread
25x Brightcloth Gloves 100x Bolt of Runecloth, 50x Gold Bar, 25x Rune Thread
25x Brightcloth Robe 125x Bolt of Runecloth, 50x Gold Bar, 25x Rune Thread
