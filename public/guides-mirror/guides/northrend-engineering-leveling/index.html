Northrend Engineering Leveling Guide 1-75

This Northrend Engineering leveling guide will show you the fastest way how to level your Northrend Engineering skill up from 1 to 75 as inexpensively as possible.

Engineering is the best combined with mining, and I highly recommend leveling these professions together because it will be a lot easier to get the needed materials if you have mining.

Approximate Materials Required

  • 260 x Cobalt Bar
  • 6 x Frostweave Cloth
  • 10 x Crystallized Earth (you can turn one Eternal Earth into 10 Crystallized Earth)
  • 40 x Crystallized Water (you can turn one Eternal Water into 10 Crystallized Water)
  • 14 x Borean Leather
  • 390 x Saronite Bar
  • 9 x Eternal Shadow
  • 2 x Eternal Water
  • 2 x Eternal Fire
  • 2 x Eternal Air
  • 8 x Titanium Bar

Engineering Tools

You will need some kind of tool to craft most engineering items. These tools are:

  • Blacksmith Hammer - Sold by Engineering or Blacksmith supply vendor.
  • Arclight Spanner - Sold by Engineering supply vendors.
  • Gyromatic Micro-Adjustor - Requires 175 skill in Vanilla Engineering to craft.

I highly recommend getting a Gnomish Army Knife to save some bag space. It replaces all of these tools, so you only have to carry one item with you.

The Gnomish Army Knife also replaces Jeweler's Kit, Virtuoso Inking Set, Runed Copper Rod, so if you have any of these in your inventory, you can throw it out.

Where to get The Gnomish Army

  • You can buy the Ultimate Gnomish Army Knife from the Auction House.

  • If you already learned Draenor engineering, you can craft one Ultimate Gnomish Army Knife for 5 Gearspring Parts. (Craft one Gearspring Parts with 15 x True Iron Ore, 15 x Blackrock Ore. It will actually create 8 Gearspring Parts).

  • You can also craft Gnomish Army Knife if you have Northrend Engineering, but it's usually more expensive to make or buy.

Leveling Engineering

You can learn Northrend Engineering from Timofey Oshenko at Dalaran (Northrend). (same NPC exists in Legion Dalaran, but he won't teach you Northrend recipes)

Gnome characters have +5 Engineering skill because of their passive Engineering Specialization, and Kul Tiran characters have +2 from the trait Jack of All Trades.

Any extra Engineering skill means recipes stay orange for more points. You can save a lot of gold by doing lower-level recipes for more points.

1 - 10

You will need around 50x Handful of Cobalt Bolts and 20x Volatile Blasting Trigger later. Make these now, and you should reach 10 or probably more. Note that when you craft Handful of Cobalt Bolts you can get 1 or 3 bolts, and the same goes for Volatile Blasting Trigger, you will get 2 each craft.

  • 35 x 1-3 Handful of Cobalt Bolts - 70 Cobalt Bar
  • 10 x 2 Volatile Blasting Trigger - 30 Cobalt Bar, 10 Crystallized Water
  • 10 x Overcharged Capacitor - 40 Cobalt Bar, 10 Crystallized Earth

Keep all of these.

10 - 15

6 x Explosive Decoy - 6 Frostweave Cloth, 18 Volatile Blasting Trigger

You might have to make more.

15 - 26

15 x Froststeel Tube - 120 Cobalt Bar, 15 Crystallized Water

Keep these. You will need them later.

26 - 30

Note: If you completed the quest for Goblin Engineering, then you can use Global Thermal Sapper Charge to level between 25-60. You can learn this recipe from Didi the Wrench.

5 x Diamond-cut Refractor Scope - 5 Froststeel Tube, 10 Handful of Cobalt Bolts

Stop making this when you reach 30.

30 - 35

15 x Nitro Boosts - 15 Tinker's Kit

You can buy the Tinker's Kit from Bryan Landers near your trainer.

35 - 45

30 x Mind Amplification Dish - 30 Tinker's Kit

You can buy the Tinker's Kit from Bryan Landers near your trainer.

45 - 50

5 x Mana Injector Kit - 60 Saronite Bar, 10 Crystallized Water

You might have to make a few more to reach 50.

50 - 55

5 x Mechanized Snow Goggles - 40 Saronite Bar, 10 Borean Leather, 5 Eternal Shadow

You might have to make a few more to reach 55.

If you are planning on crafting the portable mailbox MOLL-E, you should craft it at 54 because it's still a guaranteed skill point. The goggles are getting close to turning green, so they have a lower chance to give you a skill point at 54.

55 - 60

5 x Noise Machine - 10 Froststeel Tube, 10 Overcharged Capacitor, 40 Handful of Cobalt Bolts

60 - 74

30 x Gnomish Army Knife - 300 Saronite Bar, 30 Skinning Knife, 30 Mining Pick, 30 Blacksmithing Hammer

This recipe will be green for the last 5 points, so you will probably have to make more than 30, but it should still be cheaper than making any other items, even if you have to make 10 for 1 skill point.

You can buy Mining Pick and Blacksmith Hammer from Bryan Landers near your trainer. The Skinning Knife is sold by Ranid Glowergold.

74 - 75

I recommend making a Wormhole Generator: Northrend for the last point. This toy will create a portal that lets you teleport to Borean Tundra, Howling Fjord, Sholazar Basin, Icecrown, or Storm Peaks.

Wormhole Generator: Northrend - 8 Titanium Bar, 2 Eternal Shadow, 2 Eternal Water, 2 Eternal Fire, 2 Eternal Air
