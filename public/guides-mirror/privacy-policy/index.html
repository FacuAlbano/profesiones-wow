Privacy Policy

At www.wow-professions.com, the privacy of my visitors is of extreme importance to me. This privacy policy outlines what personal information I collect and how I use it.

What Happens When You Use The Site

When you use the site:

  • Stripe handles payment info if you subscribe (I don't see or store it)
  • Server logs record basic stuff like your IP and browser (standard web server behavior)
  • Freestar tracks you for ads
  • If you email me, I'll have your email

Payment Processing

When you buy ad-free subscriptions, I use Stripe to handle payments. I don't store your credit card info on my servers - Stripe handles all that securely. By making a payment, you're agreeing to Stripe's terms and privacy policy.

You can cancel or change your subscription anytime through the customer portal or by emailing me.

Ads

I use Freestar for ads on the site. They and their partners collect info about your visits to show you relevant ads. This includes cookies and tracking.

Log Files

Like most websites, I keep log files with your IP address, browser type, ISP, timestamps, and which pages you visit. This helps me understand how people use the site. None of this is tied to your personal identity.

Cookies

I use cookies to remember your preferences and subscription status. If you're a subscriber, cookies help me hide ads for you.

Security

I take reasonable steps to protect your info. Payment stuff goes through Stripe with proper encryption.

Third-Party Services

I use these services:

  • Stripe: Payment processing
  • Freestar: Ads
  • Resend: Sending emails

Each has their own privacy policy.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Cancel your subscription anytime
  • Contact me with questions about your data

Note: I don't store personal data on my servers - payments are handled by Stripe, and they manage subscription data.

External Links

This site links to other websites. I'm not responsible for their privacy practices or content.
